Ordinary Competence

Peace
Active resistance of chaos, disorder, emotional outbursts, and violence.
Boundaries
Setting clear limits on where you'll go, what you'll do, and the ideas you're willing to entertain.
Objective Resilience
Managing your emotions so that you do not over-react to events around you.
Analytical Depth
Focused attention that prioritizes objective evidence over assumption.
Intellectual Thoroughness
Continuous evaluation and improvement of ideas, actions, and emotional states.
Energy Conservation
Unwillingness to waste energy on actions and thoughts that have no consequence.
Pacing
Moving forward at whatever rate produces the required or desired quality of output.
Generosity
Helping others when genuinely needed, but not being taken in by charlatans.
Abundance Mindset
Using and conserving resources in a way that creates maximum benefit for everyone.
Raw Honesty
Telling the truth without spin, shade, or tone. Valuing this kind of truth in others.
Aesthetics
Creating art and beauty, in addition to functionality, whenever possible.
Passion
Creating and improving an affinity for things that reach others and stand the test of time.
