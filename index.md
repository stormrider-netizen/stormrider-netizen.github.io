This is the glass house of stormrider, aka Bill Wear.  Here I live out a lot of my daily life in public -- suitably anonymized, of course.  My plan is to record all the things that go on in my retired life.  A lot of that centers around boosting my retirement income, which isn't enough.  It doesn't fall so short that I'm panicked much, but I'm not one to sit idly by and let things deterioriate.

I'm working lots of angles: making and selling fashion denim and quilted bags; working on a self-published non-fiction book on objective thinking; working on a sci-fi novel about a rogue AI which a midsize editor has encouraged me to write and submit; and looking for a suitable job to unretire me, for a while at least.  The stakes aren't low, but they aren't sky-high, either, but it doesn't really matter: If I flail, I won't make anything good happen, so may as well approach it calmly.

You can follow my daily exploits in my blog (see the nav-bar above).  You can tip me $10 to get extra content if you like.  There's also some interesting static stuff up there; feel free to take a look.

Welcome to my little corner of the global village.
