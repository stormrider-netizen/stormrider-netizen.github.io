## Sat Aug  8 21:24:37 CDT 2026
I am in utter shock at how well just posting very mundane and honest thoughts is going. It feels weird that all people want is to see how others are coping with the mundane aspects of daily life. I didn't know that might be where this was going. 

But it's definitely headed that way. I've worked late into the evening, not shutting down before sunset as i usually do. No mandate: I actually want to do whatever this is. Having my cat mostly want to be near me has made a huge difference for me, too, and being invited to go with my wife and her best friend today has also been a real treat.

Still, I feel completely unfamiliar with this person who has emerged, and yet he fits like an old glove. Apparently I am regressing a lot of my emotional buildup, and letting it fall away without knowing exactly where it's going or how.

Screw the weather. What's really powerful for me is the absolute scale of the change.  In just a couple of days of being completely honest and forthright with myself, I am finding my way so much more cleanly, and I have no intention of stopping or changing course.  It's a completely unexpected angle for me.

There's this lingering idea that this is actually the life I really want to live: just be myself on the Internet and find a credible way of getting by doing it. Obviously, I'm just sort of stepping through this.  No clue how it came together or how it's going to get together.  Just winging it and following it.

There is clearly rare value in just telling the truth.  No pretense, no salesmanship, just putting it out there as it really is.  Who knew?
I think the anchor pint is to show enough different sides of myself to be worth curating.  That might be the hard part, but then again, that might be the easy part, too.

Actually, here's the hardest part: letting go of the showman, the ex-CIA-officer-in-his-own-mind trying to continue to put on airs and cover his tracks and disappear into the woodwork.  That won't work any more.

So what is this idea?  How do I live a live in real time on the Internet?  How do I curate it down to something compact and still real?  I don't actually know yet, but I know that actively trying to do that is what will get in the way of that.

### Journal
I didn't really do my normal journaling today. Something else took over. I fought with myself so much over the years, trying to put my best foot forward, and all of a sudden, it's a crock. And I just remembered that I wanted to teach my students about "see one, do one, teach one." I still have no idea what I'm going to say. I'm comfortable leaving it that way.  What I figure out in the moment is greater than anything I could prepare.

Today was a much better day than I realized. I got a huge head start on putting together my website, not by force, nor out of any desire to impress, but based on a desire to express. Just honesty, no filter, no posturing, no pushing for hits.
