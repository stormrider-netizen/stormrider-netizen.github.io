[Eating](https://stormrider-netizen.github.io/eating)

There's no particular reason why I started trying to change my eating habits at 67 years old, except:

* I was finding it difficult to stand from a sitting position. Not so difficult that I couldn't just write it off to old age, but difficult enough that I noticed.
* My fasting morning blood sugar was almost never below 250, if not higher. Not so high that I felt really bad, but I never felt great.
* My kidney eGFR numbers were staying below ninety. Not so bad that the kidney scans showed any damage, but I still thought about it every time I pigged out on junk food.
* I was always looking for something to drink.  Not so bad that people said anything, but I definitely had a coke bottle in my hand more often than most other people.
* I couldn't stay away from the snack table at my wife's best friend's house.  Not that she wasn't happy to see me eating, but I always felt uncomfortably stuffed when I left, and sometimes I would even try to cram some more in my pockets, as if she cared.

It's not like I was addicted to junk food, I just couldn't stay away from it, and my body and my wallet were paying a heavy price.

Yes, I was rationalizing. A lot. To my own detriment. But then, inexplicably, something changed. One morning I woke up and didn't actually want a diet soda. 
Instead, I got up, made some coffee, and sat down with my laptop. There were other things on my list for the day, and I couldn't even tell you exactly what I 
did, but I just didn't want to do all the stuff I'd been doing. I can't tell you exactly what I ate, but I can tell you I started making better choices.  In 
the store, I started passing over the ice cream and candy, and going a notch healthier. It took me awhile, but soon I was just avoiding junk food almost all the 
time. 

Then some other things started to happen, too, or maybe they preceded or precipitated this change. I can't tell you for sure, because it all sort of 
happened at once, not by any grand design or frustrated decision.

I stopped reading the news. Cold turkey. Deleted my social media apps from my home screen on my phone. Quit doomscrolling Reels, and TikTok, and Youtube Shorts.
Gave up lurking in Threads and Substack. 

And then I started sleeping better. Still sleeping in my clothes, but now covering up and turning out the lights at 9:30 or 10, whenever I got sleepy. Only using 
Solitaire or Nethack to fill dead spaces that were too short for anything serious. Started spontaneously reaching out to friends I hadn't heard from in awhile, 
by WhatsApp or Text or DM or other more normal means.

Now I'm back to building up my Website, trying to put real, meaningful content up. 

It's a thing. It's not a thing I chose, except that all the other choices just didn't seem so right any more.
