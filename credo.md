Be who you are.  
*Even a bent wire can carry a great light.*

Build for strength, not just speed.  
*The fastest horse can't carry the heaviest load.*

Distrust all claims for the one true way; there isn't one.
Divide and conquer; a house divided against itself cannot stand for long.
Do one thing well; multitasking is a lie.
Hack; we learn best by trial and error, but keep a voltmeter handy, so you don't become a memorable lesson in failure.
Keep it simple; it's cheaper and easier to carry.
Leverage everything but people (and their money); bigger hammers aren't usually the answer.
Network; you were born to connect.
Practice the Prime Directive; don't pry, judge, or imitate.
Release early, release often; the Next Big Thing often isn’t.
Say what you mean; nothing is truer than the truth.
Speak clearly, listen carefully, pay close attention; don't that read wrong.
Start small and build a little at a time; a mosaic is more beautiful than the finest concrete.
Think ahead, but don’t worship your plans; remember, today is the first day of your learning experience.
Underpromise and overdeliver; honesty breeds trust.
Use what you have; don’t dig for diamonds with a bar of gold.
